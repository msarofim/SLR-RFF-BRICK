# IMBIE 2026 (IMBIE-3) reconciled ice-sheet mass balance, 1972/1979–2023

Otosaka et al. (2026), "Mass balance of the Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets from the 1970s to 2023",
*Scientific Data* 13:1301, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-08088-0.
Data: NERC EDS UK Polar Data Centre, https://doi.org/10.5285/128c5e33-5224-4197-82f0-19dcc95b80a0
(RAMADDA entry 128c5e33-…; files dated 2026-07-06). Licence: Open Government Licence v3.0.
Downloaded 2026-09-21 by the listing in `listing.json` (`entry/get/<name>?entryid=<id>`).

Ten CSVs: {antarctica, west_antarctica, east_antarctica, antarctic_peninsula, greenland} × {Gt, mm SLE};
monthly rows; columns = date, total mass-balance rate ± σ, cumulative anomaly ± σ, SMB anomaly rate ± σ
and cumulative, dynamics anomaly rate ± σ and cumulative (see the header row of each file).
Greenland INCLUDES the peripheral glaciers and ice caps (8.0 ± 0.5 Gt/yr, ~7 %) — scope-matched to
Ladrillo's GIS target (Frederikse GrIS = ice sheet + RGI region 5).
