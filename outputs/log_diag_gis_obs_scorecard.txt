=== 3. WHAT THIS DOES TO THE 2100 FINDING -- THE HYPOTHESIS IS REFUTED ===
  The hypothesis this section was built to test: that the ISMIP6 median UNDER-runs
  observations, so that part of our 1.32x 2100 excess is the target's own slow bias
  rather than our defect. **That is refuted by its own numbers.** The median is
  1.15x the observed rate, not below it, and 11/14 models sit at or above observations.

  ==> NO CORRECTION IS AVAILABLE. The 2100 finding stands as measured: our 1.32x is
      ours. ISMIP6 clears the same gate Greve cleared and CLIMBER-X failed, so it is
      an admissible target by this repo's own standard, and weighting the 2100 term
      HIGHEST is supported by the evidence, not merely by decision-relevance.

  ONE CAVEAT ON THE GATE ITSELF, which applies equally to the Greve and CLIMBER-X
  verdicts already on record: it compares a 2016-2050 PROJECTED rate to a
  1995-2024 OBSERVED one. The projection window is later and warmer, so a
  well-behaved model SHOULD exceed the observed rate. Read that way 1.15x is modest,
  and the notable number is that 3/14 models fail to reach a PAST observed rate in a
  FUTURE window. It does not change the verdict; it does mean the gate is a floor,
  not a calibration.

  AND OUR OWN STANDING, stated against the same yardstick: our 1995-2024 rate is
  0.634 mm/yr = 1.07x observed -- better than the ISMIP6 median's 1.15x. The
  hindcast is NOT where our problem is. The shape defect at the recent end is
  ACCELERATION: 0.65x observed over 1993-2024 (section 1C). We match the LEVEL and
  the RATE and under-run the CURVATURE -- and then arrive 1.32x high at 2100.
