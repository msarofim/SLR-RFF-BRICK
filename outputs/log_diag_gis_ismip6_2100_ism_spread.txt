=== 3. VERDICT ===

  Our 2100 sits at the 56-100 percentile of the ice-sheet-model ensemble in every cell
  (5/5 at or above the median), and ABOVE THE HIGHEST OF ALL 9-14 MODELS
  in 3/5 cells (by up to 1.22x).

  ==> THE 2100 FAST BIAS IS CONFIRMED, AND STRENGTHENED, BY AN INDEPENDENT ENSEMBLE.
      The single-ISM target is NOT the explanation: NORCE-CISM sits at 0.99x the
      9-13-model median (range 0.87-1.11), a typical member, so swapping it for a
      16-model median leaves the over-prediction intact -- 1.17x becomes 1.30x.
      The two GCM cells with no PROTECT counterpart are the sharpest: we run 1.41-1.48x
      the multi-model median there, above EVERY member.

  BUT KEEP THE SCALE. The ISM structural spread at a FIXED GCM forcing is
  1.34-3.14x -- comparable to or larger than our own 1.31x offset. We are at the
  high EDGE of the ice-sheet-model range, not outside anything physical. What is damning
  is the DIRECTION being the same in 5/5 cells across 4 GCMs and 2 scenarios,
  not the magnitude.

  PRIORITY CONTEXT (Marcus 2026-08-22). Matching transient ice models is priority 4
  of 5, below volume+history, long-term commitment and melt-rate constraints; and
  test stringency scales with the number of models behind it. 16 ice-sheet models (9-14 per cell)
  over 4 GCMs is far more than the ONE this arc has been scoring against, so this test
  earns more weight than the PROTECT-only one -- but it is still GUIDANCE. This file
  emits NO loss term, NO band and NO admissible set, deliberately.

  CAVEAT THAT TRAVELS. ISMIP6's CMIP6 GrIS forcing is MARv3.9-based; PROTECT uses
  MARv3.12 / RACMO2.3p2. Part of the PROTECT-vs-ISMIP6 gap at a shared GCM is that
  RCM version, not ice-sheet structure.
