=== 4. VERDICT ===

  SICOPOLIS's year-3001 loss exceeds our FULLY-EQUILIBRATED commitment in
  5/5 cells, by 5.8-9.5x on the ssp585 cells.

  ==> THE CLIMBER-X RESULT SURVIVES AN INDEPENDENT MODEL, AND THIS ONE CLEARS THE
      OBSERVED RATE. A model that brackets the observed present-day loss rate
      (0.44-0.99 vs 0.59 mm/yr) delivers 5.8-9.5x our MAXIMUM POSSIBLE loss by year 3001
      -- 900 years of a merely HELD late-century climate, not equilibrium, and its
      high-forcing cells still accelerating. The one-model caveat on the commitment
      finding is gone.

  AND ON THE HIGH-FORCING CELLS IT IS A LOWER BOUND TWICE OVER: a HELD late-century
  climate rather than stabilised CO2, and a rate still 1.1-2.9x its early value in the
  final century.

  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY. It does not locate a threshold -- Greve's protocol holds a
  late-century climate rather than scanning stabilisation levels, so CLIMBER-X remains
  the only source here for WHERE the commitment curve turns over, and that is still
  one model. What is now multi-model is the direction and the SIZE: our linear L_eq
  is far too small, and the defect is structural.
