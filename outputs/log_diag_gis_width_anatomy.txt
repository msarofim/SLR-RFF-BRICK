[E] IS IT OUR CLIMATE ENSEMBLE RATHER THAN OUR GREENLAND? — an internal control
    Greenland is 80-91% forcing, so a narrow FaIR ensemble would produce a narrow
    Greenland band with nothing wrong in the ice sheet. THERMAL EXPANSION is the
    control: it is ~84% forcing too, shares the same FaIR configs, and has its own
    literature comparators. If our climate spread were the cause, TE would be narrow
    by the same factor.
    ssp126 @2100:  TE 0.89x lit   vs   GIS 0.49x like-for-like
    ssp245 @2100:  TE 0.81x lit   vs   GIS 0.69x like-for-like
    ssp585 @2100:  TE 0.78x lit   vs   GIS 0.92x like-for-like
    ssp126 @2150:  TE 0.90x lit   vs   GIS 0.51x like-for-like
    ssp245 @2150:  TE 0.82x lit   vs   GIS 0.74x like-for-like
    ssp585 @2150:  TE 0.80x lit   vs   GIS 0.46x like-for-like
    => if TE sits near 1x while GIS does not, the FaIR ensemble is NOT the cause and
       the missing width is Greenland's own.
