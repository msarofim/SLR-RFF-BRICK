# Quarantine 2026-10-07: van Vuuren projection arms run on Smith-2024-history climate

## 1. The bug

The vv climate cubes were regenerated on the CMIP7 basis on 2026-10-02 (`../20261002_vv_smith_history_basis/README.md`,
commit 31aa963), but the products built FROM them were not re-run. Every Ladrillo and BRICK 2.0 vv arm on disk was still
driven by the Smith-history cubes.

- **Before 2014**, the vv arms read the vv ensemble mean, which on the Smith basis sat up to 0.074 K off the calibration
  driver `ssp245harm` (1995–2014 mean 0.750 vs 0.803 °C). On CMIP7 the gap is 0.015 K. So the vv arms were mixed-basis
  even against Ladrillo's own SSP arms.
- **After 2014**, the spliced path moves +0.055 K at 2100 and +0.067 K at 2300 (median).
- **MAGICC-SLR** runs on its own climate and is unaffected; its rows are identical, which was checked (max|Δ| = 0.0).
- **The MAGICC-climate spliced arms ARE affected**, because they take the FaIR mean before 2014.

Rerun decided by Marcus on 2026-10-07 (`notes/handoff_2026-10-07b_ladrillo_package.md`, decision 1). Driver:
`run_vv_cmip7_rerun_20261007.sh arms|downstream`, gated by `python/gate_vv_cmip7_basis.py` ([VV-BASIS], mutation-tested
to fail 7/7 on the Smith copy). Log: `outputs/log_vv_cmip7_rerun_20261007.txt`. Arms: 0 failed. Downstream: 0 failed.
