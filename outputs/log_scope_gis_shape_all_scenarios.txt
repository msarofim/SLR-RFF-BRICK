=== THE VERDICT: DOES ONE k SATISFY BOTH? ===

  ssp585 arms only : best k = 3 (0.293), k=1 scores 0.497
       [the published ssp585-only scan reads 0.293 at k=3 and 0.497 at k=1 -- same 8 horizons, so directly comparable]
  cool arms only   : best k = 0.75 (0.229), k=1 scores 0.262
  all five arms    : best k = 1 (0.374)
       [NOT comparable to the published 0.293 -- 20 horizons, not 8]

  -> THE ARGMINS DISAGREE: ssp585 wants k = 3, the cool arms want k = 0.75.
     Cost of each choice to the other, in that other's own metric:
       at the cool optimum k=0.75, ssp585 scores 0.655 vs its own best 0.293  (2.23x worse)
       at the ssp585 optimum k=3, cool scores 0.677 vs its own best 0.229  (2.96x worse)
     A SCALE cannot serve both. That is the case for a state-dependent
     relaxation rate rather than another point on this ridge.

     AND THE SHIPPED MODEL IS NOT SYMMETRICALLY PLACED. At k = 1:
       cool   0.262 vs its best 0.229  -> 1.14x off
       ssp585 0.497 vs its best 0.293  -> 1.70x off
     The shipped model is already close to the cool optimum; ~all of the
     deficiency is on the ssp585 side. A correction must therefore act
     SELECTIVELY on the warm arm, which is what a state-dependent term does
     and what a scale k cannot.

     LEVERAGE OF A STATE-DEPENDENT TERM (realised loss fraction L/V0 at k=1, median draw):
       SSP5-8.5 r2300: L/V0 @2300 = 0.0561   (  2.4x the ssp126-r2300 arm)
       SSP5-8.5 x2300: L/V0 @2300 = 0.1295   (  5.6x the ssp126-r2300 arm)
       SSP1-2.6 r2300: L/V0 @2300 = 0.0230   (  1.0x the ssp126-r2300 arm)
       SSP1-2.6 x2300: L/V0 @2300 = 0.0241   (  1.0x the ssp126-r2300 arm)
       SSP2-4.5 r2300: L/V0 @2300 = 0.0318   (  1.4x the ssp126-r2300 arm)
     => a term proportional to L/V0 has 4.1x more leverage on the warmest arm
        than on any cool one -- but see the deliverable numbers below before quoting that.

     THE SAME LEVERAGE ON OUR OWN DELIVERABLE SCENARIOS (k=1):
       SSP1-2.6 ( 1.74 K @2300): L/V0 = 0.0215   (  1.0x our ssp126)
       SSP2-4.5 ( 3.15 K @2300): L/V0 = 0.0327   (  1.5x our ssp126)
       SSP5-8.5 ( 7.81 K @2300): L/V0 = 0.0753   (  3.5x our ssp126)
     => on the DELIVERABLE the selectivity is 2.3x, not 4.1x.
        The arms BRACKET our ssp585 (7.81 K) rather than matching it, so the arm
        number must not be quoted for the deliverable.

     SIZING gamma (for the offline grid, not a fit): our ssp585 reaches L/V0 = 0.075,
     so under r = r0*(1 + gamma*L_b/(k_b*V0)) a 2x late-rate boost needs gamma of order
     13 on the total-V0 basis, less by ~1/k_b on the per-basin one. The cool arms
     would then move by 0.43 of that -- which is the trade gamma has to beat, and the
     reason this is a MEASUREMENT to run, not a mechanism to assume.
