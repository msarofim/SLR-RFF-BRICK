  R2 = across-GCM variance in the ISM 2300 level explained by that route; 'rank r' is
  Spearman, which does not assume linearity. 'resid sd / ISM sd' = the fraction of the
  band's own width surviving the best linear rescale of that predictor — the IRREDUCIBLE
  share: precipitation / SMB + ISM structure + member, which this cannot separate further.

  ** n IS SMALL. ** The only arm with enough GCMs to fit is ssp585 r2300, and it has 5 — a 2-parameter fit on
  5 points. Read R2 as indicative, not as an estimate with an interval. The two COOL
  arms have 2 GCMs each and CANNOT be assessed this way at all — which includes ssp245,
  the arm where CESM2 was load-bearing in diag_gis_scorecard_logo.py.
