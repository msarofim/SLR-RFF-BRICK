====================================================================================================
VERDICT
====================================================================================================
  NOT THE COEFFICIENT. alpha is 0.10574 cm per 1e22 J against an obs-implied
  0.1096-0.1140 -- 0.93-0.97x, i.e. slightly LOW and partially offsetting.
  IT IS THE DRIVER, AND ABOUT HALF OF THAT IS DEPTH SCOPE. FaIR is 1.22x the
  0-2000 m products the target is built from, but only 1.11x IGCC's OWN
  full-depth series. The >2000 m layer is 10% of the observed trend and
  FaIR includes it while the target does not.
  ⚠ THE RESIDUAL 1.11x IS A FaIR QUESTION, NOT A BRICK ONE -- which is exactly why
    BRICK 2.0 misses by the same amount. Nothing in either sea-level model can fix it.
  ⚠ AND THE CORRECTION IS AN UPPER BOUND. Scaling the steric target by the HEAT ratio
    1.102 overstates it: the deep ocean is colder, so its thermal expansion per
    joule is SMALLER than the upper ocean's. The true scope correction lies between
    1.00 and 1.102, so the model/target ratio lies between 1.08x and 1.19x.
  ⇒ THE FAIL SURVIVES AS A WARN AT WORST, AND IT IS NOT A SEA-LEVEL DEFECT.
