  ⇒ the partition is NOT drifting steadily — it is UNDEFINED where the total is near zero
    (|rate| < 50.0 Gt/yr before ~1995; shares there run to 2.27 and -0.91) and STABLE after.
    From 2002 on it holds to about ±0.03: south ~0.58, mid ~0.23, high ~0.19.

  ⇒ SO: a shares term must be restricted to windows where the denominator is safe, and the
    cumulative 1972-2018 split (48.1/31.7/20.2) should NOT be used as the target — it mixes a
    near-balance era into a ratio. The modern rate shares are the well-posed quantity.

  What IS robust: on the MODERN shares the high basin is still dormant (0.19 vs 0.37 of
    volume, ratio ~0.51) and the mid basin still over-active (0.23 vs 0.17, ratio ~1.3 —
    lower than the cumulative 1.83, but the same sign). The design — high tapped, mid
    active — survives on either measure; only the MAGNITUDE of mid's over-activity moves.


  ⇒ the two basins the mock holds DORMANT contributed 52% of the 1972-2018 loss,
    and NW alone is the single LARGEST contributor (32%) while holding only 17% of the volume.
    The shipped A+B absorbs all of it while being driven by SOUTH-zone temperature alone, so its
    calibrated parameters are compensating for loss that came from other basins.
