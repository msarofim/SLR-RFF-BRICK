    Ladrillo's AIS component IS BRICK 2.0's: the AIS slot is never `replace!`d,
    so the shipped model runs stock MimiBRICK v2.0.0 DAIS from the depot.
    What differs is the POSTERIOR plus two per-draw reparameterisations
    (runoff line sampled along T_on; anchor-preserving T_ant map).
    => criterion (1) holds BY IDENTITY. The open question is the CALIBRATION,
       and the calibration changes are SUBSTANTIVE (calibrate_mcmc_ext.jl:33-44,
       phase-2 2026-07-20). Marcus 2026-08-25: these are important updates.
       A2  lambda / ais_gamma / ais_kappa FREED under their paleo marginals --
           they were FIXED at the medoid, so BRICK 2.0 reports ZERO uncertainty
           on the parameter that dominates the 100/150-yr pulse.
       A4  runoff line reparameterised to its identified direction (T_on, c):
           the (h0, c) posterior correlation was 0.9997 and BRICK 2.0's fitted
           onset (+0.62 C GMST) is unphysical.
       A5  an SMB EQUILIBRIUM term -- model beta_total (1979-2008 Gt/yr) vs
           area-scaled Rignot 2019 -- breaking the SMB-vs-discharge input-output
           degeneracy the posterior had pinned 34:1 tighter than either flux.
       A6  the GMST->Antarctic map sampled as a transient amplification `amp`,
           prior centred on CMIP6 PAI1 (Xie 2022), anchor preserved.
       plus ais_ocean_temperature0 freed; 25 -> 29 physical parameters.
       ⚠ A5 is what this script reads as 'the equilibrium'. If a different one
         was meant, the enumeration above is the full list to choose from.
