# Pulse→SLR headline marginals — CH₄ in CO₂-equivalent (AR6 GWP-100 = 27.0, non-fossil)

Both gases in **cm per GtCO₂(eq)**, directly comparable. CH₄ converted cm/TgCH₄ → cm/GtCO₂eq
via ×(1000/27.0) = ×37.037. Non-fossil GWP because the FaIR CH₄ pulse has no CH₄→CO₂ oxidation
(verified empirically). **Fossil CH₄ is excluded from the headline** — our fossil construction co-emits
the oxidation CO₂ as an instantaneous pulse, whereas a real fossil pulse would release it gradually over
the methane's oxidation lifetime; see headline_table_fossil_ch4.md for that sensitivity.
