6. VERDICT
================================================================================================
   1. THE MAGICC GLACIER GAP IS FIRST OF ALL A CLIMATE GAP. MAGICC's own 2300
      GMST is 0.12-0.96 K COLDER than FaIR's on the SAME emissions, and colder on
      every one of the seven markers. Driving Ladrillo's UNCHANGED glacier module
      with MAGICC's own climate raises its worst equilibrium headroom at 2300 from
      2.40 cm to 16.28 cm (6.8x), or 9.97 cm with S_eq floored at pre-industrial.
      The earlier '4x too small' compared a bound computed at FaIR's temperature
      against a drawdown computed at MAGICC's -- not like-for-like.
   2. ON ITS OWN CLIMATE THE BOUND NO LONGER RULES THE LARGEST DRAWDOWNS OUT.
      The floored bound covers MAGICC's own drawdown on 2 of the 5 markers that
      have one (vvLN, vvML) -- the two LARGEST -- and falls short on vvVL, vvL, vvHL.
      What is left is a RATE gap, not an equilibrium one: symmetric relaxation
      (R=1, the fastest this law allows) delivers 2.94 cm against 8.54 cm, i.e.
      34%. Matching MAGICC would need regrowth FASTER than melt, which the
      shared-kappa form cannot express at any R -- a structural difference, not a
      miscalibrated curve.
   3. THE CURVE IS NOT OUT OF LINE WITH ITS EXTERNAL ANCHOR. 0 of 4 GlacierMIP3
      rungs fall outside the likely range on the posterior, and the posterior sits
      ABOVE the GlacierMIP3 central at every rung (49/57/67/80 vs 37/46/63/76 %), so if anything the
      curve commits MORE loss than the anchor, not less. Lowering S_eq is not
      indicated by the anchor, and the driver-offset frame changes nothing (§3).
   4. THE 2100 LEVEL IS RATE-LIMITED, NOT EQUILIBRIUM-LIMITED. Saturation S/S_eq
      at 2100 is 0.53-0.57 (0.76-1.12 by 2300), and the instant-equilibrium ceiling
      sits 11.0-13.6 cm above the shipped median -- against a largest comparator
      gap of 4.31 cm. The curve cannot be what holds 2100 down; there is 3x more
      room in the approach than the whole gap needs.
      CROSS-CHECK: the independent single-reservoir Mengel arm reported 62%
      realised-of-committed at 2100 (outputs/mengel_hightemp_melt_summary.md),
      the same order as the 53-57% here on a different structure.
   5. SO THE TWO GAPS ARE NOT ONE FINDING. The handoff's guess that the 2100
      lowness and the 2300 drawdown share a cause in the equilibrium curve is not
      supported: the curve is high, not low, and both gaps sit in the RATE law --
      the 2100 one in how fast melt approaches S_eq, the 2300 one in whether
      regrowth may exceed melt. That is the next thing to scope, and it is the
      kappa/nu pair, not (a, b, T_off).
   ⚠ NOT SCOPED HERE: whether MAGICC's colder 2300 is right. It is a CLIMATE
      question (0.96 K at vvLN) and it now carries the glacier comparison, so it
      deserves its own check against the FaIR calib 1.6.0 forcing before any of
      this is used to argue Ladrillo's glacier module is missing a mechanism.
